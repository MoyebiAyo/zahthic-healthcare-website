### Questions And Answers

#### What is Zahthic Healthcare Solutions?

Zahthic Healthcare Solutions is a healthcare, rehabilitation, wellness, prevention, and community impact organization focused on improving health outcomes through accessible services, education, outreach, and long-term wellbeing support.

#### Is Zahthic a hospital?

No. Zahthic should not be understood as a traditional hospital. Zahthic is a healthcare solutions and impact organization focused on rehabilitation, prevention, wellness, education, home care, corporate wellness, and community health development.

#### Is Zahthic only a physiotherapy clinic?

No. Physiotherapy is an important part of Zahthic's work, but the organization also provides rehabilitation, home care support, health education, corporate wellness, community-based rehabilitation, outreach programs, and impact initiatives.

#### How do I book a consultation?

You can use the Book Consultation button on the website and complete the custom booking form. The Zahthic team will review your request and follow up with the next steps.

#### Does Zahthic offer home care physiotherapy?

Yes. Zahthic provides home care physiotherapy for people who need professional rehabilitation support in the comfort of their home.

#### Who can benefit from Zahthic's services?

Zahthic serves individuals, families, stroke survivors, people recovering from injury, older adults, caregivers, workplaces, communities, NGOs, institutions, and partners interested in health impact.

#### Does Zahthic work with organizations?

Yes. Zahthic works with corporate organizations, NGOs, educational institutions, healthcare institutions, government agencies, and development partners.

#### Can Zahthic organize a health outreach program?

Yes. Zahthic designs and supports outreach programs focused on health education, prevention, rehabilitation awareness, wellness, and community health development.

#### Can I volunteer with Zahthic?

Yes. Volunteers can apply through the Volunteer & Careers page. Opportunities may include outreach support, health education assistance, documentation, logistics, media, administration, and community engagement.

#### Can I support or sponsor Zahthic's work?

Yes. Individuals and organizations can support Zahthic through project sponsorship, SPACE Project support, CSR partnerships, in-kind support, or donation inquiries.

#### Does Zahthic publish service prices?

No public pricing is currently listed. Service needs vary, so the team provides guidance after reviewing the specific request.

#### Where is Zahthic located?

Zahthic is based in Orlu / Owerri, Imo State, Nigeria. Full office address and map details should be added before launch.
