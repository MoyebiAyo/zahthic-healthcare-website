# Zahthic Healthcare Solutions - Post-Launch Maintenance Plan

## First 7 Days

- Check all forms daily and confirm records reach the production CRM/email inbox.
- Confirm WhatsApp handoffs route to the official Zahthic number.
- Verify analytics page views and conversion events are recording.
- Review mobile pages on real phones, especially Home, Services, Contact, SPACE, Support, and Admin.
- Replace any remaining placeholders with approved partner logos, testimonials, impact numbers, and contact details.
- Monitor performance and fix large images, layout shifts, or console errors quickly.

## First 30 Days

- Publish at least two approved blog or impact updates.
- Review search snippets and update meta descriptions if needed.
- Confirm donation/payment flow is legally approved before taking live payments.
- Export CRM inquiries weekly and classify them by booking, partnership, volunteer, support, newsletter, and media.
- Review accessibility with keyboard-only navigation and at least one screen reader pass.
- Check broken links and redirects after the domain is connected.

## Monthly

- Update blog, project, media, and recognition content.
- Verify all forms, WhatsApp links, chat, newsletter, analytics, and CRM integrations.
- Review site speed and compress any new images before upload.
- Back up CMS content and exported form records.
- Audit published claims, impact numbers, and partner logos for accuracy and permission.
- Review security updates for dependencies and hosting.

## Quarterly

- Refresh homepage impact stories and featured project.
- Review service pages for current offerings and clinical accuracy.
- Update FAQ based on recurring inquiries.
- Review accessibility, SEO, and performance reports.
- Run stakeholder review with leadership, program team, and admin users.

## Ownership Matrix

| Area | Owner |
| --- | --- |
| Brand/content approvals | Zahthic leadership |
| Medical/service accuracy | Zahthic healthcare lead |
| Blog/project publishing | Communications/admin team |
| CRM and inquiry follow-up | Operations/admin team |
| Analytics review | Marketing or digital lead |
| Technical maintenance | Web developer/hosting manager |
| Donations and compliance | Finance/legal lead |

## Emergency Checklist

If the site breaks after launch:

1. Check hosting status and recent deployment.
2. Verify domain/DNS and SSL.
3. Test the homepage and contact form.
4. Roll back to the previous stable deployment if a new release caused the issue.
5. Notify Zahthic leadership with the issue, impact, action taken, and next update time.
